How it works

Four steps, all inside the side panel next to your Flow tab.

1. Paste your prompts

Put one prompt in each block, with a blank line between blocks.

2. Pick your settings

Choose the image ratio, model, how many pictures per prompt, and how fast to go.

3. Press Start

The extension fills in each prompt on Flow, presses Generate and waits for the result.

4. Collect your files

Pictures download into a folder with your own prefix and numbers, like scene-001.

What you get

Built for people who make many images at a time.

Your own file names

Set a prefix and a starting number. Files are saved in a folder you choose inside Downloads.

Pause and resume

Stop any time and carry on later. If the browser closes, your queue is still there.

Automatic retry

If a prompt fails, the extension can try it again, and lists the failed ones so you can redo them.

Speed choices

Go one at a time, or send several prompts at once. Faster speeds may trigger Flow's own limits, so the panel warns you.

Reference pictures

Attach start and end frames, ingredients, or named characters to your prompts.

Stays in your browser

Your prompts and pictures stay on your computer. See the privacy policy.

Questions

Is this made by Google?

No. Flow Batch Generator is an independent tool and is not made, endorsed or supported by Google. Google Flow is a product of Google LLC.

Does it work with videos?

Text to Image is the main, tested mode. Text to Video, Frame to Video and Ingredients to Video are included in beta. Video uses your Flow credits, so test with one prompt first.

Will Flow ever block me for going too fast?

Flow can flag unusually fast use. The side panel offers slower speeds and warns you about the faster ones. Choose the speed you are comfortable with.

Why does it ask for the "debugger" permission?

Flow only accepts real mouse clicks on its Generate button. The permission lets the extension send that click on your Flow tab. It is only used on Flow pages.

Where are my pictures saved?

In your normal Downloads folder, inside a sub-folder whose name you choose.
