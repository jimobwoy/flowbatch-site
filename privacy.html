Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what Flow Batch Generator ("the extension", "we") collects, why, and what your choices are. Contact: jimoifeanyi1@gmail.com.

What stays on your computer

Your prompt lists, queue, settings and any pictures you attach (start frames, ingredients, characters) are stored in your own browser. We do not receive them. The pictures the extension downloads go to your Downloads folder.

What we collect to run accounts

  • Your name (if you give one) and email address, to create and identify your account.
  • A password, stored only as a scrambled hash, never in readable form.
  • Subscription details: trial start, paid-until date and payment history (plan, amount, date, Paystack reference).
  • Simple usage counts: whether the extension is open, whether a batch is running, and how many prompts and pictures were processed. We do not collect the text of your prompts or the pictures themselves.
  • Basic technical logs on our server (such as the time of a sign-in) to keep the service secure.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. Your card or bank details are entered on Paystack's pages and are never seen or stored by us. Paystack tells us whether a payment succeeded.

How we use this information

To sign you in, check that your trial or subscription is active, process payments, answer your messages, prevent abuse, and understand overall usage. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Browser permissions

  • Flow websites (labs.google, flow.google.com): the extension only works on these pages and reads them to fill in prompts and detect finished pictures.
  • debugger: used on the Flow tab to send the real click Flow requires on its Generate button.
  • downloads: saves your pictures with the names you choose.
  • storage, alarms, sidePanel, scripting, activeTab: save your settings and queue, keep long batches running, show the side panel, and operate on the tab you are using.
  • Access to our server: to sign in and check your subscription.

We do not use these permissions to read your other websites, and we do not collect your browsing history.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run the extension: our hosting and database providers, and Paystack for payments. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account and payment records while your account exists and as long as needed for accounting. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing jimoifeanyi1@gmail.com. Uninstalling the extension removes its data from your browser.

Children

The extension is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.

Not affiliated with Google

Flow Batch Generator is an independent product and is not made or endorsed by Google.
